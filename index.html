          How the score works

          For each tested link, the model adds the extra estimated travel time across affected trips and a 30-minute penalty for each trip that becomes unreachable. The candidates are the most-used links on the sampled routes.

          Trip sampling uses one direction per pair of configured points. The rankings apply to this sample and the closures in effect when it ran.

            Map gestures and keyboard

            Drag or use the arrow keys to pan. Pinch, press + or −, or hold Ctrl while scrolling to zoom. The mouse button beside the map style switches between scroll-to-pan and scroll-to-zoom. Press Enter to select the map center while picking an endpoint, and Escape to cancel.

            Offline maps and online services

            Offline map uses local streets, labels, search indexes, and road data. Worldwide search and Stadia’s Satellite and Hybrid styles use internet access. Select a map pin to open nearby Google Street View when connected.
